Computer Vision · Undergraduate Thesis

ConvNeXt–KAN Image Classification

Can a Kolmogorov–Arnold Network (KAN) improve image classification when the pretrained backbone stays fixed?

Approach
I kept the feature extractor fixed and varied the classification head to isolate the effect of the architectural change.
Takeaway
KAN delivered a small accuracy gain at a higher computational cost. I built a side-by-side demo to show how the two heads classify the same image and how their confidence scores differ.

Machine Learning · Audit Analytics

PDAM Tariff Classification

Which customer records should PDAM Surabaya check first for possible tariff mismatches?

Approach
I removed tariff-derived fields to avoid leakage and used stratified validation and macro F1 because the largest tariff class was about 133 times the smallest.
Takeaway
Logistic Regression edged out Random Forest, and building category was the strongest predictor. The model was evaluated against historical labels, so disagreements are leads for manual verification before any tariff change.

Annual Diabetes Cases Prediction

Could a neural network estimate annual diabetes cases in Indonesia from demographic and economic indicators?

Approach

Takeaway

Does reading a movie review in both directions improve sentiment classification enough to justify the extra runtime?

East Java Poverty Determinants

What explains differences in poverty rates across East Java, and how much does geography matter to the model?

Approach
I checked whether nearby districts had similar poverty rates, then compared OLS, spatial regression and a Spatial Random Forest using 2024 BPS indicators.

Geostatistics · Regression

East Java GWR Poverty Analysis

Do poverty indicators have the same relationships with poverty across all of East Java’s districts and cities?

Approach
I fitted a global OLS baseline and tested Gaussian, bisquare and tricube kernels on 2022 data from 38 districts and cities.
Takeaway
Bisquare GWR reduced AIC from 166.38 to 157.26 and raised model-fit R² from 0.808 to 0.823. Mapping coefficients showed where relationships with factors like schooling and drinking-water access were stronger or weaker.

Undergraduate Thesis · Computer Vision

Implementation of ConvNeXt with a Kolmogorov–Arnold Network-Based Classification Head for Image Classification

A comparative study of ConvNeXt–KAN and ConvNeXt–MLP on CIFAR-10

Using a frozen pretrained ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone, I trained classification heads based on Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks (KAN) and multilayer perceptrons (MLP), evaluating both across 10 independent runs. ConvNeXt–KAN reached 94.84% mean test accuracy versus 94.65% for ConvNeXt–MLP, a modest improvement with higher computational cost.

To make the models accessible beyond the research report, I also developed a Dockerized Flask demo showing side-by-side predictions and confidence scores.
